# Phi Beta Sigma Collegiate Pathways Foundation Design System

## 1. Brand Identity
- **Brand Name:** Phi Beta Sigma Collegiate Pathways Foundation
- **Tagline:** Advancing Scholars. Funding Access.
- **Positioning Statement:** The PBS Foundation is a mission-driven 501(c)(3) that helps collegiate members of Phi Beta Sigma access transformational conferences, leadership opportunities, and educational experiences through targeted travel assistance.
- **Brand Promise:** Honor scholarship, brotherhood, and service through clear, trustworthy, uplifting communications.
- **Voice & Tone:** Academic, inspiring, respectful, service-oriented, community-centered. Sound credible enough for donors and warm enough for students and alumni.
- **Audience Priorities:** Collegiate members, alumni, chapter leaders, donors, volunteers, and community partners.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary**
  - `Royal Sigma Blue` — `#003DA5`
  - `Foundation White` — `#FFFFFF`
- **Secondary**
  - `Heritage Silver` — `#B7BDC7`
  - `Deep Navy` — `#0F2747`
- **Accent**
  - `Scholar Gold` — `#D6A74A`
  - `Success Green` — `#2F7D4A`
- **Neutral**
  - `Midnight Ink` — `#172033`
  - `Slate` — `#5A6577`
  - `Mist` — `#F3F6FA`
  - `Pearl` — `#E9EDF3`
- **Semantic**
  - `Success` — `#1F7A45`
  - `Warning` — `#C98A1C`
  - `Error` — `#B42318`
  - `Info` — `#2563EB`
- **Usage Notes:** Royal blue and white should be unmistakable. Silver supports the fraternity-inspired palette with a dignified institutional feel. Gold is reserved for scholarships, milestones, and celebration moments.

## 3. Typography
- **Heading Font:** Merriweather Bold
- **Body Font:** Source Sans 3
- **Code/Data Font:** IBM Plex Mono
- **Scale**
  - `xs` — 12px
  - `sm` — 14px
  - `base` — 16px
  - `lg` — 18px
  - `xl` — 24px
  - `2xl` — 30px
- **Typographic Rules:** Headings should feel formal and educational. Body copy should remain highly readable for forms, outreach emails, newsletters, and donor materials. Use sentence case, not all caps, for most interface and website elements.

## 4. Spacing & Layout
- **Grid System:** 12-column desktop grid, 6-column tablet grid, 4-column mobile grid
- **Breakpoints**
  - `sm` — 640px
  - `md` — 768px
  - `lg` — 1024px
  - `xl` — 1280px
- **Spacing Scale:** 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 40, 56
- **Max Widths**
  - Website shell — 1200px
  - Reading column — 700px
  - Application forms — 840px
- **Layout Guidance:** Prioritize clarity, inclusion, and trust. Use structured sections for eligibility, application steps, donor impact, board leadership, and testimonials.

## 5. Iconography
- **Icon Library:** Heroicons or Lucide
- **Sizes:** 16px, 20px, 24px, 28px
- **Stroke Weight:** 1.75px
- **Style:** Clean outline icons with occasional filled badges for awards or milestones
- **Usage Notes:** Preferred themes are education, travel, leadership, service, and community. Icons should feel collegiate and polished, not playful or childish.

## 6. Motion & Animation
- **Duration Tokens**
  - `fast` — 120ms
  - `base` — 200ms
  - `slow` — 300ms
- **Easing Curves**
  - `standard` — `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`
  - `gentle` — `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`
  - `exit` — `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)`
- **Transition Patterns:** Soft fades, accordion reveals for FAQ content, progress-step transitions for application flow
- **Motion Principle:** Motion should feel supportive and calm. Avoid aggressive movement or flashy transitions that compete with the mission.

## 7. Component Patterns
- **Buttons**
  - Primary: Royal blue background, white text
  - Secondary: White background, royal blue border, royal blue text
  - Supportive CTA: Silver or navy text link for secondary tasks
- **Cards:** White cards with blue headings, 12px radius, subtle border, optional scholarship impact stat
- **Forms:** Step-based application forms with progress indicator, helper text, and compassionate validation language
- **Navigation:** About, Programs, Apply, Donate, Board, Contact
- **Testimonials & Impact Blocks:** Pair student outcomes with chapter/alumni support quotes
- **Donation Modules:** Use trust signals, concise impact statements, and simple giving choices

## 8. Content Voice
- **Writing Principles**
  - Center opportunity, access, and leadership development
  - Write with dignity and encouragement
  - Be explicit about eligibility, process, and donor impact
  - Use fraternity and nonprofit language carefully and respectfully
- **Do Examples**
  - “The Foundation helps collegiate brothers reach conferences and leadership spaces that expand educational and professional opportunity.”
  - “Every contribution supports access, exposure, and personal growth for the next generation of leaders.”
- **Don't Examples**
  - “We hand out free travel money.”
  - “Apply now before funds disappear.”
- **Terminology Glossary**
  - `Travel assistance` — financial support for approved conference participation
  - `Collegiate member` — eligible student member of Phi Beta Sigma
  - `Leadership development` — growth through conferences, workshops, and exposure
  - `Donor impact` — measurable outcomes created through giving

## 9. Anti-Patterns
- Do not replace royal blue with generic bright blue variants.
- Do not use harsh black-heavy layouts that overpower the white-and-blue identity.
- Do not use fraternity symbols carelessly or in overcrowded decorative patterns.
- Do not write copy that sounds transactional, manipulative, or overly promotional.
- Do not make application steps ambiguous; clarity is part of trust.
- Do not use loud athletic styling; this is a scholarship and leadership brand, not a sports brand.
